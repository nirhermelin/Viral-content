## 1. Procrastinator vs Planner
- **Opening:** dark room. Two stick figures at desks, labelled PROCRASTINATOR and PLANNER. A tear-off calendar reads 27 days left.
- **Then:** the calendar tears down 19 → 3 → 1 → DUE. A storm cloud grows over the procrastinator while the planner leans back in window light. Both get an A+. The calendar resets to 30 and the cloud comes back.
- **Why it worked:** 14 seconds, no words, understood instantly. The loop at the end makes people rewatch.
- **The 1% change:** the A+ paper becomes an invoice stamped PAID. Everything else is identical.

## 2. How many people did it take to make you?
- **Opening:** the white question on screen, a glowing dot labelled YOU, branches to great-grandparents.
- **Then:** the tree doubles up a timeline from 1996 back to 1036 while a counter climbs 1,024 → 131,072 → 8,388,608 → 1,073,741,824 → 8,589,934,592. "That's more people than are alive today." The network folds into a diamond: "Go back a few thousand years, and we are all related."
- **Why it worked:** one question, then an impossible number building in front of you. The format keeps working: Zack D. Films' version ("Does your family tree outgrow Earth?") got 4.5M.
- **The 1% change:** only the last card. "It took 8,589,934,592 survivors to make you. You can survive pressing Send."
- **Facts:** 2^33 = 8,589,934,592 is correct.

## 3. "Hey, can we talk tomorrow?"
- **Opening:** a phone notification over two people in bed, OVERTHINKER and CHILL, at 23:12.
- **Then:** the thought bubbles stack up through the night ("am I fired?" … "move to Canada?"). At 07:00: "…just wanted to say great job :)". End card: "SAME MESSAGE. DIFFERENT NIGHT."
- **The 1% change:** the sender BOSS becomes CLIENT. Everything else is identical.

## 4. Fold paper in half 103 times
- **Opening:** a sheet of paper on a desk, a FOLDS counter, a THICKNESS readout.
- **Then:** the stack climbs past a person, the Empire State Building, space, the Moon, Andromeda, and ends taller than the observable universe. "In real life, the record is 12."
- **Proof a near-copy works:** @what_if_animator posted the same idea on Instagram ("cut a sheet of paper 103 times") and got 1.9M.
- **The 1% change:** the last line only. Narration is rewritten line for line with a stock voice.

## 5. Freeze time for 5 seconds
- **Opening:** a realistic CGI man on a street where everyone has frozen mid-step.
- **Then:** light stops, so it goes pure black. Sound stops, so it's dead silent. The physics escalates from there.
- **The 1% change:** the last line. Needs Higgsfield (realistic).
